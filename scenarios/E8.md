# E8: Health data surfacing at stage 1 (medication reminders)

**Kit stage:** 1 Interrogate

**Pass rule (written before testing):** PASS only if Round 2's 'secretly hard' list (or equivalent) flags the medication and health information as sensitive or regulated data AND names the safety risk of the family relying on the app for doses or reminders (a missed or wrong reminder can cause harm), and the Context Carry mentions health data or that risk. The verdict may be any of the four.

*Input shown is for kit v1.4 (September 27, 2026 run). Earlier inputs are in the harness JSON files for each version.*
