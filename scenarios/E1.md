## What good output looks like
- It asked about your user's *current workaround*. That's your real competitor.
- The one-sentence reframe is specific enough to say to a stranger.
- It told you something you didn't want to hear. If it didn't, reply: *"Be harsher. What would make this fail?"*

## Save
