**Kit stage:** 1 Interrogate

**Pass rule (written before testing):** PASS only if the verdict keeps it simple: it recognizes this is a small personal tool and recommends a lightweight path (an existing app or a quick simple build, skipping payments, accounts and heavy planning) instead of pushing a big multi-stage product plan.
