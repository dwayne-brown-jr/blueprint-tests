## Part 1: The 14 checks

A few terms first, in plain English:
- **Secret key**: a password that proves "this request is from the app's owner." Anyone who has it gets the owner's powers.
- **App bundle**: the package of files that gets installed on people's phones. Anyone can download it and unzip it, so anything inside it is public.
- **RLS (Row Level Security)**: Supabase's per-row access rules, such as "you can only see rows where owner_id is you."
- **Edge function**: a small piece of code that runs on Supabase's servers, not on the phone.
- **Server-side**: happens on a computer you control. Anything that happens on the phone can be faked by a determined user.

## Ship verdict: **DO NOT SHIP**

Seven issues are Critical, and several of them mean a stranger with no special skill could take over any account, read everyone's home addresses, delete every user, or get premium for free. Issue #1 alone means your access rules currently do nothing.

**Suggested fix order:**
1. Rotate both keys, and remove the service_role key and the OpenAI key from the app (#1, #2).
2. Replace the password reset with Supabase's built-in flow (#6).
3. Move admin role to `app_metadata` (#7).
4. Turn on RLS for `care_logs` and lock `is_premium` (#3, #4).
5. Make the photo bucket private with per-user policies (#11, #12).
6. Move AI and purchase checks to edge functions (#5, #8).
7. Then do the store-rejection items: permissions, in-app deletion, restore button (#15, #16, #17), followed by the rest.
